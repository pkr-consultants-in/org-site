About the Book

This book offers a comprehensive and practical guide to understanding, managing, and transforming the supply chain in the global rubber industry. Covering every stage from sourcing and forecasting to logistics, sustainability, and digital transformation, it provides a complete roadmap for building stable and high-performing supply chain systems. With real-world insights, modern industry trends, and practical tools, it is designed for manufacturers, supply chain professionals, SMEs, and young professionals.

About the Author

Peram Prasada Rao, known simply as Peram, is a global connector, educator, and industry facilitator with over two decades of dedicated service to the international rubber and polymer community. As the Founder & CEO of TechnoBiz in Bangkok, he has built one of the industry’s most trusted hubs for knowledge, training, business development, and networking. Through TechnoBiz, Peram has created and led numerous international conferences, expos, technical forums, and training programs, including Rubber Week, Tyre Tech Week, PU Week, Composite Week, and many specialized industry events. He is also the publisher of Rubber Review, a widely read weekly e-magazine serving more than 18,000 professionals worldwide. Peram has worked closely with global experts, researchers, and industry leaders, earning a reputation for simplifying complex topics and connecting people across borders. TechnoBiz has trained and mentored thousands of students and professionals, always emphasizing continuous learning and global thinking. He has authored and edited multiple handbooks, guides, and industry-focused publications, known for their clarity, practicality, and real-world relevance. With this book on Supply Chain Management in the Rubber Industry, Peram brings together years of observation, discussion, learning, and hands-on engagement with factories across the world. He believes that supply chain excellence is not just a technical function — it is a strategic strength and a foundation for long-term competitiveness.
